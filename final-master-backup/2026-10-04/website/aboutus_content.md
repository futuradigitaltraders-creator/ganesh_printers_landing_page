Our Journey
In 1976, in Vathalakundu, on Pillaimar Street, this printing business was started by
my father, Mr. Veerabhathiran, the founder. It was established as the third printing press
in the town under the name Ganesan Press, using a treadle printing machine.
At that time, my father skillfully printed everything in single-color format, from wedding
invitations to bill books and notices.
I began learning the printing trade at the age of 12, working alongside my father and my
brothers. By the time I finished school in 1992, I had learned the traditional art of
typesetting using lead letters, arranging each letter by hand. With my father’s guidance, I
learned the printing business completely. Even today, he remains my guru and mentor in
this profession.
Between 1994 and 1996, I stayed in Sivakasi to learn multi-color printing techniques. I
also learned how to produce calendars, diaries, books, and school notebooks.
In the early 2000s, we became the first printing company in Vathalakundu to offer all
types of multi-color printing, including daily and monthly calendars. Over time, we built a
strong customer base across South Tamil Nadu.
In 2000, we expanded our business to Kodaikanal. At that time, most hotels and businesses
there used only single-color treadle machine printing. We introduced the modern trend of
multi-color printing, bringing a new style and quality to the region.
Growth and Expansion
In 2004, when flex banner printing became a big trend in the printing industry, we
learned this new technology in Madurai, Chennai, and Bengaluru. We added this service
to our business and renamed our company Sri Ganesh Printers.
We invested in more computers and printing machines, learned to design and type
content digitally, and created multi-color designs. We then took our business to the next
level by offering printing through offset machines and flex banner machines using
outsourcing methods.
In 2005, we started a separate business in Sivakasi (Viswanatham) under the name Sri
Annai Traders, focusing only on the production of daily and monthly calendars and
diaries. Even today, this unit operates successfully during the calendar season. We continue
to introduce new and updated calendar designs every year, and our collections are
available up to the 2026 editions. We strongly believe that change is the only constant.
In 2007, we expanded further in Vathalakundu by purchasing an offset printing machine
and additional computers. We also increased our team to 7–10 employees, which helped
us grow our printing services even more.
In 2008, we opened a new branch in Kodaikanal, located in the town’s main area on Anna
Salai. It was our first shop in that location, and it has been running successfully for 17
years (as of 2025).
Technology Upgrades and New Services
In 2010, we purchased our own flex printing machine in Vathalakundu and
combined it with our offset printing press, which helped us expand our services even
further. Since then, we have proudly remained the only company in Vathalakundu and
Kodaikanal offering both offset printing and flex printing under one roof.
Continuing our growth, in 2014, we introduced an instant multi-color digital printing
machine. This allowed us to provide immediate multi-color prints on boards, stickers, and
art paper up to a size of 12 inches × 45 inches.
As the next milestone, in 2015, when LED boards began gaining popularity in the printing
and advertising industry, we quickly learned the technology and added it to our services. We
successfully started designing and producing LED sign boards.
In 2017, we introduced a digital die-cutting machine at our Kodaikanal branch. With this,
we can create stickers, labels, and boards in custom shapes and designs, even for
single-piece orders.
In 2021, we launched Sri Ganesh Advertisements, a dedicated company focused on
advertising services. We introduced a special advertising vehicle equipped with flex
banners, speakers, and microphone facilities for promotional campaigns.
We also manufacture eco-friendly traffic sign boards such as No Parking, No Entry, and
Parking boards. These are installed using iron frames and pipes in approved locations
that do not disturb traffic. All installations are done with proper municipal and traffic police
permission, and we display advertisements for clients along with these boards.
In 2023, we reached another milestone in flex printing by introducing a 6-color eco-friendly
inkjet printing machine at our Kodaikanal branch.
We also introduced UV Direct Printing Technology, which allows us to print directly on
materials such as acrylic sheets, foam sheets, ACP sheets, sunpack sheets, star flex,
and backlight boards. This modern technology helps us deliver high-quality and
long-lasting prints on a wide range of surfaces.

We accept even small quantity orders such as 25, 50, 100, or 200 pieces. Printing options
